# Project 4 — AI in GRC *(planned)*

Demonstrates using AI to assist GRC work **without ceding control design to the model** — the human owns the design and boundaries; the AI accelerates the repetitive parts and is always rechecked.

**Planned contents**
- **AI-assisted classification** — propose data classifications over the synthetic catalog, with the model required to explain *why* each recommendation was made, then a human-defined recheck/validation step that catches over- and under-classification.
- **Control-evidence summarization** — draft plain-language summaries of collected evidence for reviewers, flagged as draft-for-human-review.
- **Guardrails doc** — where AI is and isn't allowed in the process, the recheck step, and how misclassification is caught (based on real lessons: AI first-pass classification during internal audits can confidently mislabel).

Principle: *the human owns the design; with the design we set the boundaries the controls operate within, and AI works inside them.*
