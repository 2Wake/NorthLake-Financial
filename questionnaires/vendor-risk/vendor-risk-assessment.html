Methodology & references

This tool follows the standard third-party risk approach rather than a bespoke question set: rate the vendor's inherent risk (data sensitivity × access × criticality), score control effectiveness across eight domains, and derive residual risk from the two via a risk matrix. The questions are a condensed, domain-organized distillation of established industry questionnaires — the kind of tailored, risk-tiered assessment a TPRM program sends in place of firing a 300-question form at every vendor.

Modeled on and compatible with:

Shared Assessments SIG / SIG Lite CSA CAIQ → Cloud Controls Matrix NIST SP 800-161 (C-SCRM) NIST 800-53 SR family ISO/IEC 27036

Vendor risk-tiering follows the Shared Assessments model. See the questionnaire reference guide for when to use each standard. Illustrative tool on a fictional vendor; all data synthetic.
