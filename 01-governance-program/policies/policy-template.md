**How to use this template**

1. Copy the Confluence page into the Policies area and rename it `POL-xx Title` using the ID from the Policy Register.
2. Replace every placeholder (shown here in `[brackets]`). Delete this guidance and anything in *italics*.
3. Keep every heading. If a section doesn't apply, write "Not applicable" and say why.
4. Follow STD-01 Policy Management Standard for versioning, review and approval.
