The company

Empire City Taxi Co.

A fictional NYC taxi & for-hire mobility operator running a cloud-native platform on Google Cloud — rider and driver apps, dispatch, and card payments — pursuing SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 while meeting PCI DSS, GDPR/CCPA, and NYC TLC obligations.

Industry
Taxi & for-hire mobility (NYC)
Hosting
Google Cloud (GKE, BigQuery, Cloud SQL)
Data
Rider & driver PII, cardholder data, trip data
Posture
SOC 2 Type II · ISO 27001:2022 (in progress) · PCI DSS · GDPR/CCPA

Integrity note. Empire City Taxi Co. is a fictional company. The operational trip data is the public NYC TLC dataset; every name, driver, rider, card, number, and piece of "evidence" is synthetic, created to demonstrate GRC practice. Framework mappings are illustrative and defer to official reference tables (AICPA, ISO, NIST) in a real program.
